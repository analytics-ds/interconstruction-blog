## Why are the fees reduced for new-build?

The gap comes from a single item: **transfer duties**. For older homes this duty reaches 5.80 to 6.30% of the price depending on the department (many raised it in 2025); for new-build it falls to 0.715%. The notary's pay and the other fees stay the same. That is why an identical home costs far less in fees when it is new.

## How are notary fees calculated?

The calculator adds four items:

- **Transfer duties**: 0.715% of the price for new-build, 5.80 to 6.30% for older homes depending on the department.
- **Notary's fees**: a national sliding scale (3.870%, then 1.596%, 1.064% and 0.799%), plus 20% VAT.
- **Property security contribution**: 0.10% of the price.
- **Disbursements**: a few hundred euros of advanced costs.

### Worked example

For a **new apartment at 300,000 €**, notary fees are around **6,200 €**, about 2.1% of the price: 2,145 € of transfer duties, 3,350 € of fees including VAT, 300 € of property security contribution and 400 € of disbursements. The same property as an older home would cost nearly **23,800 €** in fees (about 7.9%), a gap of more than 17,000 € explained solely by the transfer duties.

## How to cut the bill further?

Beyond the new-build advantage, the notary can grant up to **20% off** their fees above 100,000 €. Also include these fees from the budgeting stage: they are usually not financed by the loan. ## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How much are notary fees for new-build?</summary>

For new-build, notary fees represent around 2 to 3% of the purchase price, versus 7 to 8% for older homes. On a 300,000 € home, that is about 6,000 to 9,000 € for new-build, versus 21,000 to 24,000 € for an older property.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How are notary fees calculated?</summary>

They add four items: transfer duties (0.715% for new-build, around 5.80% for older homes), the property security contribution (0.10%), the notary's fees on a sliding scale, and disbursements. Only the transfer duties explain the gap between new-build and older homes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are notary fees negotiable?</summary>

Since 2021, the notary can grant up to 20% off their fees on the share of the price above 100,000 €. Transfer duties and the property security contribution, however, are fixed and non-negotiable.

</details>
