## Why buy new-build in Île-de-France

Buying **new-build** appeals to a growing number of households looking for an efficient, maintenance-free home. Beyond comfort, buying a **new home** unlocks financial and legal benefits that older properties do not offer. The Île-de-France region concentrates a large share of new developments, driven by steady demand.

### A favourable tax framework

The first advantage is **reduced notary fees**, lowered to 2-3% of the price versus 7-8% for older homes, a gap our [notary fees calculator](/en/calculators/notary-fees/) helps you quantify. In some zones, VAT drops to 5.5% instead of 20%. A two-year property tax exemption is also common.

## The steps of an off-plan purchase

New-build is most often bought off-plan, through a **VEFA** (sale in a future state of completion). This process is secured by a strict legal framework: reservation contract, staged fund calls by progress and completion guarantees.

| Step | Description | Indicative timing |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Reservation | Contract signing and deposit | Day 0 |
| Notarised deed | Signing at the notary | 2 to 4 months |
| Fund calls | Staged payments by progress | 12 to 24 months |
| Delivery | Handover and snagging | End of works |

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What are the benefits of buying new-build?</summary>

Buying new-build offers reduced notary fees (2 to 3% versus 7 to 8% for older homes), extended builder guarantees, energy performance compliant with the RE2020 standard and, in eligible zones, reduced VAT of 5.5%. A new home requires no renovation work for several years.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which guarantees protect a new-build buyer?</summary>

Three legal guarantees apply: the completion guarantee (1 year), the two-year guarantee on equipment, and the ten-year structural guarantee. The financial completion guarantee also secures delivery of the home.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does it take to buy a new-build home?</summary>

Between reservation and delivery, expect around 12 to 24 months for an off-plan (VEFA) purchase, as the property is built during this period. The notarised deed is signed a few months after reservation.

</details>
