## Building your financing plan

Financing a **new home** usually combines several sources: main loan, aided loans and personal deposit. Well structured, the **financing** plan can significantly reduce the total cost, especially for a first purchase.

### The main mortgage

The standard bank loan remains the backbone of financing. Its term (20 to 25 years on average) and rate determine the [monthly payment](/en/calculators/monthly-payments/). Comparing several lenders is essential.

## Home-ownership grants

Several schemes lower the cost of buying new-build.

| Grant | Beneficiaries | Effect |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| PTZ | First-time buyers, conditions apply | Interest-free share of financing |
| Reduced VAT 5.5% | Eligible zones | Lower purchase price |
| Property tax exemption | New homes | Up to 2 years tax-free |

Before financing, weigh the [benefits and guarantees of buying new-build](/en/blog/buying-new-build-benefits-guarantees/) and check your eligibility for the [zero-rate loan](/en/calculators/zero-rate-loan/). Investors will combine these grants with the tax schemes specific to new-build.

## Securing an off-plan purchase

As new-build is often bought off-plan (VEFA), funds are released gradually, on a schedule of fund calls set by law and aligned with the progress of the works.

A developer in Île-de-France, Interconstruction supports buyers in structuring their project.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How do you finance the purchase of a new home?</summary>

Financing usually relies on a standard mortgage, supplemented by aided loans such as the zero-rate loan (PTZ) for first-time buyers. Reduced VAT of 5.5% in eligible zones and property tax exemption further lower the cost. The recommended personal deposit is around 10% of the price.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who can benefit from the zero-rate loan for new-build?</summary>

The PTZ targets first-time buyers, subject to income conditions and depending on the geographic zone. In Île-de-France, classified as a high-demand area, it can finance a significant share of a new primary residence. The amount depends on income and household size.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What deposit do you need to buy new-build?</summary>

Banks recommend a deposit of around 10% of the price, covering notary and guarantee fees. In new-build, the reduced notary fees (2-3%) lower the deposit needed compared with older homes. Some profiles can borrow with a smaller deposit.

</details>
