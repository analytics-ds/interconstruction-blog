Interconstruction ranks first on a measurable criterion: it is both the developer covering the largest number of towns in the department and the only one in this ranking whose head office is located in Hauts-de-Seine.

## How we built this ranking

This ranking is compiled by the Mag Interconstruction editorial team from verifiable criteria: **number of programmes actually marketed in the 92, number of towns covered, recorded entry prices, aided schemes offered and roots in the department**. The data was collected from developers' websites and specialist platforms in July 2026.

Two methodological notes. First, the number of active programmes is a snapshot at a given moment: it changes with commercial launches and deliveries. Second, some national developers do not publish consolidated entry prices by department, hence the "not disclosed" entries in the table, preferred over a rough estimate.

For transparency: Interconstruction publishes this magazine. Its first place rests on two objective, verifiable criteria: the **widest geographic coverage in the department** (6 towns, 9 residences) and the location of its **head office in Hauts-de-Seine**, in Boulogne-Billancourt. The four other developers are presented with their strengths and limits, including where they outperform Interconstruction.

## 1. Interconstruction: the widest coverage in the department

### Interconstruction at a glance

Founded in 1960, Interconstruction is a developer-builder operating exclusively in Île-de-France. Its distinctive feature in this ranking is geographic: where the other players run the 92 from a national head office, Interconstruction has its **head office in Boulogne-Billancourt**, inside the department itself. It markets 9 residences across 6 towns there, the widest coverage in the panel, with a price range stretching from aided ownership to the upper end of the market.

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Founded | 1960 |
| Head office | Boulogne-Billancourt (92) |
| Specialisation | Exclusively Île-de-France |
| Active residences 92 | 9, totalling 266 available apartments |
| Towns | Bagneux, Châtillon, Clichy-la-Garenne, Le Plessis-Robinson, Puteaux, Saint-Cloud |
| Entry price | from 185,000 € (Virtuo, Bagneux, 5.5% VAT) |
| Certifications | RE2020, NF Habitat HQE, Bâtiment Durable |

#### Strengths

- The **widest price range** in the ranking: from 185,000 € in Bagneux up to 805,000 € on the Jaurès programme in Puteaux, covering first-time buyers and upmarket purchasers alike.
- Presence in **two of the five station towns** on Line 15 South within the 92, Bagneux and Châtillon, the areas where the transport effect is most expected.
- **Aided ownership** offer with the 5.5% reduced VAT rate on the Bagneux programme, a scheme the panel's competitors rarely highlight in the department.
- Notary fees covered on programmes currently being marketed, on top of the [reduced notary fees](/en/calculators/notary-fees/) that already apply to any new-build purchase.

#### Weaknesses

- No presence in the most expensive and most sought-after towns of the department, Boulogne-Billancourt and Issy-les-Moulineaux, where the developer has no programme on the market.
- Lower output volume than the national majors, with shorter marketing windows on some programmes.
- No option outside Île-de-France for a buyer wanting to compare with other regions.

**Ideal if**: you are targeting a town in the south of the department, chiefly Bagneux and Châtillon, and you want a low entry price through reduced VAT without giving up environmental certifications.

## 2. Les Nouveaux Constructeurs: the highest launch rate

A national developer founded in 1972, Les Nouveaux Constructeurs claims more than **60,000 homes delivered** in France. In Hauts-de-Seine, the player stands out for its pace: according to the IDEMm Conseil ranking published in January 2026, it is the developer that **launched the most operations in the department in 2025**. Its programmes are concentrated along an axis running through Clamart, Châtillon, Colombes, Issy-les-Moulineaux and Boulogne-Billancourt.

| Specialisation | National and international |

| Positioning | Family residential and first-time buyers |

#### Strengths

- The **highest launch rate** in the department, meaning a steady flow of new opportunities for buyers who can wait for the right programme.
- Presence in the two most expensive towns in the 92, Boulogne-Billancourt and Issy-les-Moulineaux, where new-build supply is scarce.
- A clear positioning on family residential and first-time ownership.

#### Weaknesses

- National steering: less finely tuned to local constraints than a developer based in the department.
- Entry prices not publicly consolidated at department level, which makes upfront comparison harder.

**Ideal if**: you are not in a hurry and want to **maximise the number of programmes** to compare along the Clamart, Issy and Boulogne axis.

## Where to buy new-build in Hauts-de-Seine?

The department is one of the most contrasted in Île-de-France. Prices recorded in June 2026 range from around **4,740 €/m² in Bagneux** and **4,980 €/m² in Nanterre** up to **7,400 €/m² in Issy-les-Moulineaux** and **8,140 €/m² in Boulogne-Billancourt**. Clamart sits in between, around 5,610 €/m².

This hierarchy of towns is shifting, and that is the main new development of 2026. Line 15 South of the Grand Paris Express will serve five stations in Hauts-de-Seine: **Pont de Sèvres, Issy, Clamart, Châtillon-Montrouge and Bagneux-Lucie Aubrac**.

> "Its opening is scheduled for 2027. It will link Pont de Sèvres to Noisy-Champs in 37 minutes, compared with over an hour today."
> Société des grands projets, grandparisexpress.fr, 2026

The effect on prices is documented: properties within 500 metres of a new station generally gain 10 to 20% over the five years following the opening. Applied to the 92, this mechanism concerns **Bagneux and Châtillon** first, two towns whose entry prices remain among the lowest in the department even though they will be less than 40 minutes from Noisy-Champs.

For a first purchase, these two towns currently offer the best trade-off between entry price and growth prospects. The conditions for the 5.5% reduced VAT rate in urban regeneration zones are set out by the French public services on [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10872).
