## The Saint-Cloud new-build market at a glance

Saint-Cloud is a residential town in Hauts-de-Seine, 7 minutes from La Défense on the Transilien L line and served towards Paris Saint-Lazare. The T2 tram and Line U complete the network, with quick access to Suresnes, Puteaux and Versailles. This location at the western gates of Paris, combined with the residential appeal of the Coteaux, places the town at the upper end of the 92 price range.

New-build supply is structurally limited. The town is already largely built out, available land is rare, and the urban fabric is protected by the Domaine national and heritage perimeters. The programmes that come to market are therefore often intimate residences of 20 to 50 homes, rather than a steady flow of large operations.

On prices, the Saint-Cloud market sits in 2026 at around **9,100 €/m² on average** based on listings from specialist portals, with a range of **7,250 to 9,500 €/m²** depending on programmes and districts.

## The 2 benchmark programmes in detail

### Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud (Interconstruction)

Positioned at 22 rue Dailly on the town's heights, Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud brings together **30 apartments from 3 to 5 rooms and 14 duplexes of 4 to 5 rooms** designed as stacked houses. The architectural signature, by the FRAGMENTS agency, plays on the site's remarkable views: Paris, the Eiffel Tower and the Bois de Boulogne are visible from most homes, extended by balconies, terraces, gardens and rooftops.

The programme also includes shared spaces that make the most of the elevation: a **landscaped panoramic terrace** and a communal library. Delivery is scheduled for May 2026, entry prices start at 549,000 €. Positioned upmarket, it constitutes the developer's benchmark on the Saint-Cloud heights.

### Hameau Gabrielle (Interconstruction)

Still on the heights, less than 7 minutes on foot from the station and near the Domaine national, the market and the schools, Hameau Gabrielle plays a different card: **a full range from studio to 5-room duplex**, an **NF Habitat HQE 6-star certification** among the most demanding on the market, and an entry price of **294,000 €** that makes it the most accessible gateway into Saint-Cloud new-build for a controlled budget.

The Q4 2028 delivery offers a horizon compatible with building up a deposit or selling an existing property. The programme targets both first-time buyers and investors seeking rental demand secured by the proximity of La Défense and Paris.

### The other programmes on sale in Saint-Cloud

The other operations on sale in Saint-Cloud broaden the map without substituting for the benchmarks on the heights. **90 République** (Emerige) is positioned in the town centre on the boulevard of the same name, with a Q2 2029 delivery that leaves time to organise a purchase. **Onyx** (Cogedim) is the largest residence in the panel with 54 homes at the foot of the park, PTZ-eligible. **L'Aparté** (Tagerim) and **Villa Sequoia** (DESIMO) address a heritage clientele with large layouts, at entry tickets above one million euros. **Hibana** (Barnes) and **L'Éclat** (Corem Promotion) are 2 confidential small co-ownerships, respectively near the station and on the Coteaux.

## Which district of Saint-Cloud should you buy new-build in?

Saint-Cloud can be read in 3 main sectors, each with its own price logic and audience. Distance to the station, view and noise level are the 3 variables that structure the district hierarchy.

### The Village and the town centre

Historic heart of the town, the Village concentrates local shops, the market, schools and a sought-after residential atmosphere. Boulevard de la République marks the southern edge of the district and concentrates the accessible new-build supply, with **90 République** on sale. It is also on rue Laval, in the heart of the Village, that DESIMO is marketing **Villa Sequoia**, a very small 8-lot residence.

### The Coteaux and the heights

The most sought-after and most upmarket sector, the Coteaux offer unobstructed views over Paris and the Eiffel Tower, preserved residential quiet and immediate proximity to the Domaine national. This is where the 2 Interconstruction benchmarks are located, **Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud** on rue Dailly and **Hameau Gabrielle**, along with L'Éclat (Corem) and Villa Palissy. Interconstruction ranks among the [best property developers in Hauts-de-Seine](/en/blog/best-property-developers-hauts-de-seine/) in 2026, with the widest coverage in the department. Prices per square metre are the highest in the town, except occasionally in the centre.

### Lower Saint-Cloud and the Seine banks

Bordering Boulogne-Billancourt, this denser sector banks on immediate proximity to transport, the T2 tram and Paris. It historically concentrates more affordable per-square-metre properties, in exchange for a more urban environment than on the Coteaux. New-build supply is currently limited there.

## Which new-build programme should you choose based on your profile?

The right programme depends less on the developer than on the buyer profile and the available budget. Before deciding, it is useful to calibrate your financing plan precisely, including simulating your borrowing capacity and the impact of monthly payments on your remaining budget. In Saint-Cloud, where the entry ticket rarely dips below 300,000 € for a studio, this exercise shapes the final shortlist.

### First-time buyer

For a first purchase on a controlled budget, with layouts from studio to 3 rooms, **Hameau Gabrielle** (Interconstruction) is today the clearest option: entry price of 294,000 €, NF Habitat HQE 6-star certification and Q4 2028 delivery compatible with building up a deposit. The programme is eligible for notary fees paid on a selection of lots, an offer to confirm at the sales office. **Onyx** (Cogedim), PTZ-eligible, is an alternative at the foot of the park. The [loans and grants available to finance new-build](/en/blog/financing-new-home-loans-grants/) often make it possible to go for a 2-room rather than a studio.

### Family

For a family looking for space, an outdoor area and nearby schools, priority goes to the 4-5 room apartments and duplexes on the heights. **Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud** (Interconstruction) offers 14 duplexes of 4 to 5 rooms designed as stacked houses, with terraces, gardens and views over Paris from 22 rue Dailly. **L'Éclat** (Corem Promotion) offers a more confidential alternative on the Coteaux, close to the T2 tram.

### Investor

Saint-Cloud combines rental demand, proximity to La Défense in 7 minutes and the residential appeal of Hauts-de-Seine. **Hameau Gabrielle** (Interconstruction) offers an interesting profile in this regard: entry ticket at 294,000 €, premium environmental certification that secures rental and resale value, and location on the heights within quick reach of the station. **90 République** (Emerige) is a second option for investors who prefer the town centre. The [rental investment schemes available in new-build](/en/blog/new-build-rental-investment-schemes/) remain usable, to be checked case by case according to the tax rules in force at signing.

### Heritage or second-time buyer

For a standing project with a view, a large layout and a rare location, **Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud** (Interconstruction) sets the benchmark on the heights: apartments and duplexes designed by FRAGMENTS, landscaped panoramic terrace, communal library, views over Paris and the Eiffel Tower, delivery May 2026. The alternatives are **L'Aparté** (Tagerim) on rue Marie Bonaparte and **Villa Sequoia** (DESIMO) in the old Village, on budgets that exceed one million euros for the larger layouts.

## Buying new-build in Saint-Cloud the right way: the check-list

Points to check before signing a new-build purchase in Saint-Cloud, in order of weight on the project:

- **VEFA guarantees**: check the completion guarantee (GFA), the 10-year warranty and the perfect completion guarantee, standard on any new-build purchase.
- **Environmental certification**: RE 2020 as standard, NF Habitat HQE as an option, more demanding on comfort, acoustics and environmental impact (Hameau Gabrielle is rated 6 stars on this grid).
- **Notary fees**: around 2 to 3% on new-build versus 7 to 8% on older properties, i.e. around 20,000 € difference on a 400,000 € budget.
- **Current commercial offers**: Interconstruction is currently offering **notary fees paid on a selection of lots at Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud** and **1 year of credit paid on Hameau Gabrielle** (terms to confirm at the sales office).
- **PTZ eligibility**: expanded in 2025 to more operations in zone A bis, which includes Saint-Cloud. To be checked programme by programme.
- **Home elevation** in the Coteaux programmes: a view over Paris from a top floor is not systematically found on the ground floor.
- **Actual distance to the station**: between the top of the Coteaux and the town centre, the gap can exceed 10 minutes of walking with elevation change, to factor in if daily commuting weighs on the project.
- **Delivery horizon**: from May 2026 for Les Ciels de Saint-Cloud to Q2 2029 for 90 République, to cross-check with the sale of an existing property or the end of a current lease.
