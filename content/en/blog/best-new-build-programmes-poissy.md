Four new-build programmes are being marketed in Poissy in 2026: **Les Ateliers de Poissy, Lisle Lumière, Les Jardins des Vergers and Villa Antoinette**. Les Ateliers de Poissy stands out on a verifiable timing criterion: it is the only one delivered in February 2027, the very month the RER E reaches the town, while the other three arrive between fifteen and twenty-four months later.

> **Article summary**
> 1. Four new-build programmes in Poissy in 2026: Les Ateliers de Poissy (Interconstruction, AKERA, SEMAP), Lisle Lumière (Brownfields), Les Jardins des Vergers (Les Nouveaux Constructeurs) and Villa Antoinette (Icade).
> 2. Entry prices range from 161,000 € (Villa Antoinette) to 210,700 € (Les Jardins des Vergers).
> 3. The RER E serves Poissy from 28 February 2027: only Les Ateliers de Poissy is delivered by then, the others in 2028 and 2029.
> 4. Two locations compete: the town centre with direct station access, and the Rouget-de-Lisle eco-district with its 1.7-hectare park.

## How we built this comparison

This comparison is compiled by the Mag Interconstruction editorial team from verifiable criteria: **recorded entry prices, unit types offered, delivery date, location and ownership schemes**. The data comes from developers' websites and specialist platforms, collected in July 2026.

The scope covers programmes **actually being marketed** in Poissy at that date. The Instants Nature programme, delivered in the first quarter of 2025, has been excluded: it is no longer available for sale, which makes it irrelevant to a purchase decision.

For transparency: Interconstruction is a co-developer of Les Ateliers de Poissy and publishes this magazine. Its leading position rests on two objective criteria, the **earliest delivery date** and the **only town-centre location** with direct station access. On entry price, however, Villa Antoinette clearly beats Les Ateliers de Poissy, and this comparison says so plainly.

## 1. Les Ateliers de Poissy: the only one delivered as the RER E arrives

### The programme at a glance

Located on rue Paul Codos, in the Saint-Louis district right in the town centre, **Les Ateliers de Poissy** comprises 139 apartments for sale, from studios to 6-room units. The programme is part of the transformation of the Paul Codos block and stands out for its delivery date, February 2027, which coincides with the RER E entering service in Poissy.

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Developers | Interconstruction, AKERA, SEMAP |
| Homes | 139 apartments for sale |
| Unit types | Studio to 6 rooms |
| Neighbourhood | Saint-Louis, town centre, Paul Codos block |
| Entry price | from 209,000 € |
| Delivery | February 2027 |
| Certifications | RE 2020, NF Habitat HQE, BiodiverCity label |
| Schemes | Bail Réel Solidaire, zero-interest loan, notary fees covered |
| Sales status | 79% of units reserved |

#### Strengths

- **Delivery in February 2027**, fifteen months ahead of the next programme. It is the only one of the four available when the RER E enters service.
- **Direct access to Poissy station** from the programme, whereas the other three require a walk or a bus ride. A decisive advantage for daily use of the RER A and then the RER E.
- The **widest range** in the comparison, from studios to 6-room units, the only programme offering family-sized homes of this scale.
- **Triple certification** RE 2020, NF Habitat HQE and the BiodiverCity label, the latter being rare on operations of this size.
- Eligibility for the **Bail Réel Solidaire** scheme, which separates land from building ownership and sharply reduces the acquisition cost for households below income thresholds.

#### Weaknesses

- Entry price at 209,000 €, that is **48,000 € more than Villa Antoinette**, the cheapest in the comparison.
- **79% of units already reserved**, which significantly narrows the choice of remaining layouts and floors.
- A dense town-centre location, without the green setting offered by the eco-district programmes.

**Ideal if**: you want to move in quickly, you plan to use the train daily, or you are looking for a large family home. It is also the only option if the Bail Réel Solidaire scheme is central to your project. ## 2. Lisle Lumière: the programme at the heart of the park

### The programme at a glance

Developed by **Brownfields**, Lisle Lumière sits at the heart of the Rouget-de-Lisle eco-district and offers apartments from studios to 5-room duplexes. Its main argument is the setting: the homes overlook a **1.7-hectare wooded park**, with generous balconies and terraces extending the living spaces.

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Developer | Brownfields |
| Unit types | Studio to 5-room duplex |
| Neighbourhood | Rouget-de-Lisle eco-district |
| Entry price | from 204,000 € |
| Delivery | Q2 2028 |
| Key strength | Views over a 1.7-hectare wooded park |
| Connections | La Défense in 20 min, Charles de Gaulle-Étoile in 26 min via RER A |

#### Strengths

- The **highest-quality landscaped setting** of the four, with open views over the park.
- Entry price at 204,000 €, slightly below Les Ateliers de Poissy.
- Competitive journey times towards western Paris, 20 minutes to La Défense on the RER A.

#### Weaknesses

- Delivery in the second quarter of 2028, that is **more than a year after** the RER E arrives.
- Located in an eco-district still taking shape, whose shops and facilities are not all delivered.
- No unit type beyond 5 rooms.

**Ideal if**: quality of setting matters more than moving in quickly and you accept waiting until 2028.

## 3. Les Jardins des Vergers: on the edge of the forest

### The programme at a glance

Developed by **Les Nouveaux Constructeurs**, this programme at 13 rue du Docteur Schweitzer combines apartments and intermediate housing units with private entrances. Its distinctive feature is its position **500 metres from the Saint-Germain-en-Laye forest**, with individual gardens, loggias and terraces depending on the unit.

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Developer | Les Nouveaux Constructeurs |
| Unit types | 2 to 5-room duplex |
| Address | 13 rue du Docteur Schweitzer |
| Entry price | from 210,700 € (2-room unit of 42.56 m²) |
| Top of range | 396,900 € (5-room duplex of 90.8 m²) |
| Delivery | Q3 2028 |
| Schemes | 5.5% VAT, zero-interest loan, LMNP, LLI |
| Connections | Forest 500 m away, station 12 min by bus, A14 motorway 1.2 km |

#### Strengths

- The **widest tax range** in the comparison: reduced 5.5% VAT, zero-interest loan, furnished rental status and intermediate rental housing, which suits owner-occupiers and investors alike.
- Intermediate housing units with **private entrances**, a format close to a townhouse and rare in apartment blocks.
- Immediate proximity to the Saint-Germain-en-Laye forest.

#### Weaknesses

- The **highest entry price** of the four, at 210,700 €, that is around 4,950 €/m² on the 2-room unit.
- Delivery in the third quarter of 2028.
- Station reachable in 12 minutes by bus, so without the direct walking access of the town centre.

**Ideal if**: you are investing and want to weigh up several tax regimes, or you are looking for a home with a private entrance on the edge of the forest.

### The programme at a glance

#### Strengths

- The **lowest entry price** in the comparison, at 161,000 €, that is 48,000 € below Les Ateliers de Poissy.
- A **small-scale operation**, 69 homes across two buildings, for those who prefer smaller developments.
- An outdoor space for every apartment, without exception.

#### Weaknesses

- **Delivery in the first quarter of 2029**, the most distant of the four, two years after the RER E arrives.
- The financial carrying period, between current rent and first repayments, stretches over nearly three years.
- An eco-district still under development at the delivery date.

**Ideal if**: budget is your deciding criterion and your moving horizon falls in 2029. Before committing, measure your [borrowing capacity](/en/calculators/borrowing-capacity/) factoring in the carrying period.

## Why buy in Poissy in 2026?

Poissy shows an average price of around **3,791 €/m²** as of 1 May 2026, all property types combined. New-build programmes sit above that, between 4,400 and 5,000 €/m² depending on unit type, a normal gap that pays for construction guarantees, RE 2020 standards and the [reduced notary fees](/en/calculators/notary-fees/) of new-build.

The decisive factor in 2026 is rail. Poissy is already served by the RER A and Line J. On top of that comes the westward extension of the RER E, whose date is now fixed.

> "The RER E will arrive in Mantes-la-Jolie on 28 February 2027, a wait of nearly 40 years finally rewarded."
> Town of Mantes-la-Jolie, manteslajolie.fr, 2026

The extension covers 55 kilometres, including the redevelopment of the existing line from Houilles-Carrières to Mantes-la-Jolie via Poissy. Service will start on a reduced timetable in 2027 before ramping up in 2029. For a buyer, this means a home delivered in 2027 benefits from the new service as soon as they move in, whereas a home delivered in 2029 already has it priced into its purchase cost.

That is where the trade-off between the four programmes plays out. The conditions of the Bail Réel Solidaire scheme are set out on [BoRiS, the French government's official platform](https://boris.beta.gouv.fr/tout-savoir-sur-le-bail-reel-solidaire-brs).
