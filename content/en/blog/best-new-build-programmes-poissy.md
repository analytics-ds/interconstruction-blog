Crossed by the Seine and backing onto the Saint-Germain-en-Laye forest, Poissy combines remarkable heritage, including Le Corbusier's Villa Savoye listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site, with urban renewal reshaping several neighbourhoods. This article reviews the criteria that set a good new-build programme apart in this Yvelines town, then details four benchmark developments currently on the market.

Among the new-build programmes available in Poissy in 2026, four stand out: **Les Ateliers de Poissy by Interconstruction, Lisle Lumière by Brownfields, Les Jardins des Vergers by Les Nouveaux Constructeurs and Villa Antoinette by Icade**. Les Ateliers de Poissy stands out on a verifiable timing criterion: it is the only one delivered in February 2027, the very month the RER E reaches the town, while the other three arrive between fifteen and twenty-four months later.

## Why buy a new-build programme in Poissy?

### A setting between the Seine and the forest

Poissy occupies a rare position in the outer suburbs, between the **banks of the Seine** and the **Saint-Germain-en-Laye forest**, whose edge lies a few hundred metres from several new-build programmes. The town is home to the Villa Savoye, a manifesto of the Modern Movement built by Le Corbusier between 1928 and 1931, giving it architectural renown reaching far beyond the Yvelines.

The **Saint-Louis district**, right in the town centre, is undergoing an urban renewal operation reshaping its built fabric and its shops. It is within this perimeter that the Les Ateliers de Poissy programme is located.

### Rail links strengthened by the RER E

Poissy is already served by the **RER A** and by **Line J** towards Saint-Lazare. The station puts La Défense around 20 minutes away and Charles de Gaulle-Étoile 26 minutes away on the RER A.

The new development of 2026 is the westward extension of the RER E, whose date is now fixed.

> "The RER E will arrive in Mantes-la-Jolie on 28 February 2027, a wait of nearly 40 years finally rewarded."
> Town of Mantes-la-Jolie, manteslajolie.fr, 2026

The extension covers 55 kilometres and includes the redevelopment of the existing line from Houilles-Carrières to Mantes-la-Jolie via Poissy. Service will start on a reduced timetable in 2027 before ramping up in 2029. The Tram 13 Express is set to complete this arrangement in the area.

### A new-build market driven by urban renewal

Poissy shows an average price of around **3,791 €/m²** as of 1 May 2026, all property types combined. New-build programmes sit above that, between 4,400 and 5,000 €/m² depending on unit type, a gap that pays for construction guarantees, RE 2020 standards and the [reduced notary fees](/en/calculators/notary-fees/) of new-build.

New-build supply concentrates on two poles: the town centre around the Saint-Louis district, and the Rouget-de-Lisle eco-district, which alone hosts two of the four selected programmes.

## How do you recognise a good new-build programme in Poissy?

Seven criteria allow developments to be separated objectively. They form the grid used in this comparison.

| Criterion | What to check | Why it matters in Poissy |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Town centre, eco-district or outskirts | Determines access to existing shops and facilities |
| Distance to station | Access on foot, by bus, actual time | Criterion no. 1 with the RER E arriving in February 2027 |
| Certifications | RE 2020, NF Habitat HQE, BiodiverCity | Conditions future charges and resale value |
| Finishes | Materials, outdoor spaces, shared services | Strongly differentiates developments at similar prices |
| Unit types | From studio to 6 rooms | A wide range adapts as the household evolves |
| Guarantees | Completion, two-year, ten-year | Secures an off-plan purchase over several years |
| Schemes | PTZ, reduced VAT, Bail Réel Solidaire | Can change the acquisition cost by tens of thousands of euros |

Two of these criteria carry more weight than the others in Poissy. **Actual distance to the station** first, because the benefit of the RER E depends directly on walkable access. **Delivery date** next, since it determines whether the buyer enjoys the new service from the moment they move in or has already paid for it in the purchase price.

## 1. Les Ateliers de Poissy (Interconstruction): the town-centre benchmark

### The programme at a glance

Developed by **Interconstruction**, [Les Ateliers de Poissy](https://www.interconstruction.fr/ile-de-france/78/poissy/les-ateliers-de-poissy/) sits on rue Paul Codos, in the Saint-Louis district right in the town centre. The programme comprises 139 apartments for sale, from studios to 6-room units, and is part of the transformation of the Paul Codos block. Its delivery date, February 2027, coincides with the RER E entering service in Poissy.

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Developer | Interconstruction |
| Homes | 139 apartments for sale |
| Unit types | Studio to 6 rooms |
| Neighbourhood | Saint-Louis, town centre, Paul Codos block |
| Entry price | from 209,000 € |
| Delivery | February 2027 |
| Certifications | RE 2020, NF Habitat HQE, BiodiverCity label |
| Schemes | Bail Réel Solidaire, zero-interest loan, notary fees covered |
| Sales status | 79% of units reserved |

#### Strengths

- **Delivery in February 2027**, fifteen months ahead of the next programme. It is the only one of the four available when the RER E enters service.
- **Direct access to Poissy station** from the programme, whereas the other three require a walk or a bus ride. A decisive advantage for daily use of the RER A and then the RER E.
- The **widest range** in the comparison, from studios to 6-room units, the only programme offering family-sized homes of this scale.
- **Triple certification** RE 2020, NF Habitat HQE and the BiodiverCity label, the latter being rare on operations of this size.
- Eligibility for the **Bail Réel Solidaire** scheme, which separates land from building ownership and sharply reduces the acquisition cost for households below income thresholds.
- Uncommon shared amenities: rooftop shared garden, library, equipped bike store and an artwork built into the building.

#### Weaknesses

- Entry price at 209,000 €, that is **48,000 € more than Villa Antoinette**, the cheapest in the comparison.
- **79% of units already reserved**, which significantly narrows the choice of remaining layouts and floors.
- A dense town-centre location, without the green setting offered by the eco-district programmes.

**Ideal if**: you want to move in quickly, you plan to use the train daily, or you are looking for a large family home. It is also the only option if the Bail Réel Solidaire scheme is central to your project. ## What budget and what support for a new-build apartment in Poissy?

Beyond the headline price, several mechanisms reduce the real cost of a new-build purchase. Not all apply to every programme, hence the value of checking them development by development.

| Scheme | Scope | To check |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced notary fees | 2 to 3% of the price against 7 to 8% for older property | Applies to any off-plan purchase |
| Zero-interest loan | Up to 50% of the amount depending on income | Reserved for first-time buyers, below income thresholds |
| Reduced 5.5% VAT | Replaces the 20% rate | Only in eligible areas, to be confirmed per programme |
| Bail Réel Solidaire | Separation of land and building ownership | Below income thresholds, offered on Les Ateliers de Poissy |
| Property tax exemption | Up to 2 years after completion | Depends on the local council's decision, to be checked with the town hall |

Two points deserve direct verification with the developer. The **reduced 5.5% VAT** applies only to certain perimeters: the Les Jardins des Vergers programme advertises it, which confirms that eligible areas exist in Poissy, but it does not extend to the whole town. The **Bail Réel Solidaire** scheme, for its part, is offered only on Les Ateliers de Poissy among the four selected programmes.

Before committing, measure your [borrowing capacity](/en/calculators/borrowing-capacity/) factoring in the carrying period between reservation and delivery, which can approach three years on programmes delivered in 2029. Then weigh the [benefits of buying new-build](/en/blog/buying-new-build-benefits-guarantees/) and check your eligibility for the [loans and grants to finance a new home](/en/blog/financing-new-home-loans-grants/). If your plan is a rental investment, our guide to [rental investment schemes in new-build](/en/blog/new-build-rental-investment-schemes/) sets out the conditions of each regime. The conditions of the Bail Réel Solidaire scheme are set out on [BoRiS, the French government's official platform](https://boris.beta.gouv.fr/tout-savoir-sur-le-bail-reel-solidaire-brs).
