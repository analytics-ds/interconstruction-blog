## 1. Interconstruction: the exclusive Île-de-France specialist

### Interconstruction at a glance

Founded in 1960, Interconstruction is a developer-builder that has delivered more than **20,000 homes**, exclusively in Île-de-France. That is its hallmark: where the other players in the ranking operate nationally, Interconstruction focuses all its expertise on the region, and notably on the Yvelines, where it has long been established (Poissy, Voisins-le-Bretonneux, Mantes-la-Jolie, Guyancourt, Maisons-Laffitte). The developer also stands out for a strong aided-ownership offer, opening new-build to tighter budgets.

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Founded | 1960 |
| Specialisation | Exclusively Île-de-France |
| Homes delivered | 20,000+ |
| Active programmes (78) | 3 (Conflans-Sainte-Honorine, Poissy, Voisins-le-Bretonneux) |
| Entry price | from ~158,000 € via BRS / 5.5% VAT |
| Aided schemes | 5.5% VAT, Bail Réel Solidaire, zero-rate loan |

#### Strengths

- The only **100% Île-de-France** developer in the ranking: fine knowledge of the towns, local plans and transport of the Yvelines.
- Extensive **aided-ownership** offer (Bail Réel Solidaire, reduced 5.5% VAT), with some of the most accessible entry prices.
- **Diverse** products: apartments, houses, social housing, student residences, coliving, urban renewal.
- Over 60 years of experience and backing from the Eaglestone group.

#### Weaknesses

- Fewer simultaneous programmes than the national majors.
- Presence concentrated in Île-de-France: no options outside the region.

**Ideal if**: you want a developer that truly masters the Yvelines, and you want to access new-build through an aided scheme (BRS, 5.5% VAT) without compromising on quality.

## Where to buy new-build in the Yvelines?

New-build prices vary widely by town. **Poissy and Sartrouville** offer entry levels around 4,500 to 5,000 €/m², supported by transport extensions (RER A, Eole). **Saint-Germain-en-Laye** sits between 5,200 and 6,500 €/m², while **Versailles** exceeds 7,500 €/m² on premium programmes.

For a first purchase, **Conflans-Sainte-Honorine, Poissy and Montigny-le-Bretonneux** offer a good price/accessibility trade-off. Before deciding, weigh the [benefits of buying new-build](/en/blog/buying-new-build-benefits-guarantees/) and estimate your [borrowing capacity](/en/calculators/borrowing-capacity/). Aided-ownership schemes (5.5% VAT in ANRU zones, Bail Réel Solidaire) are detailed by the public authorities on [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10872).

On budget, remember that new-build benefits from [reduced notary fees](/en/calculators/notary-fees/) (2 to 3% versus 7 to 8% for older homes) and that rental investment remains relevant in a high-demand department like the Yvelines.
